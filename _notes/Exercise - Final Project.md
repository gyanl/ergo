### Project Ideas 
See [[Project Ideas]] for some possible project ideas, or propose your own.

### Proposal
*Due by 3 Nov*
*Extended deadline - Due by 13 Nov*

Describe the general problem area - what is the ergonomic problem you have identified? Why does it need to be redesigned? Describe your approach and what you will do over the next month? Break down week by week.

### Grading
| **Facets**                           | **Maximum Points** | **Evaluation Description**                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Punctuality                          | 2                  | Submitting proposal (1) and final deliverable (1) by due date.                                                                                                                         |
| Project Concept                      | 3                  | Impactfulness, clarity, and relevance of the ergonomic problem or context selected. Evaluated based on proposal.                                                                       |
| Application of Principles            | 5                  | Application of ergonomic principles to critique an existing design and identify issues.                                                                                                |
| Prototyping and Evaluation           | 5                  | Ability to translate ergonomic insights into tangible improvements, Testing with users to evaluate usability or comfort.                                                               |
| Documentation of process and outcome | 5                  | Clear and effective presentation of the work done during the project, including your research, prototypes and findings, overall coherence between research, design, and final outcome. |
| **Total**                            | **20**             |                                                                                                                                                                                        |
