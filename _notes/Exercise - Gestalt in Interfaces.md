### Activity (15 min)

- Find an app or website that you think is poorly designed. 
- Identify which gestalt principles are used or violated in the design.
- Redesign the layout using pen and paper in 5 minutes using Gestalt principles to improve perception.

### Debrief (5 min)

- We'll go over the sketches and identify which principles were applied and how.
    - What principle did you find most powerful in your redesign?
    - Where do you see _violations_ of Gestalt principles in apps you use every day?
